About the app

  • Read all eight Family Pledge verses
  • Choose from many languages
  • Personalize background themes
  • Optional background music
  • Adjust text size and card opacity

No account or sign-in is required. Preferences (language, theme, text size) are stored on your device.

Quick tips

  1. Open the app to read the pledges on the home screen.
  2. Tap the settings button (bottom right) for Language, Background, Music, and Live adjustments.
  3. Live adjustments let you change text size and card opacity while previewing the verses.
