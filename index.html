About the app

  • Read all eight Family Pledge verses in 20 languages
  • Korean / romanised recitation: listen, follow the active verse, and jump by tapping a verse
  • Family Pledge Quiz — several modes, progress on your device, optional reminders, and an optional public leaderboard
  • Browse a large song lyrics library (search, language filters, and chords)
  • Listen to holy songs, piano, guitar, chamber, jazz, and LA Family Church playlists
  • Tap the mini-player title to switch between Music and the Family Pledge text (or lyrics)
  • Photo gallery
  • Heavenly calendar: holy days, anniversaries, Blessing days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times
  • Optional local reminders on iOS and Android
  • About page with version and download links
  • Personalize background themes, text size, and card clarity
  • Also available on the web at familypledge.app

You can use the app without an account. Preferences, reminder settings, and quiz scores stay on your device. Sign-in (Apple, Google, or email code) is optional if you want to join the public quiz leaderboard with a display name. You can leave the board or delete an account in the app (Account → Delete account). The App may send anonymous usage analytics to help us improve it — see the Privacy Policy.

Quick tips

  1. Start from Home to open Family Pledge, Quiz, Calendar, Music, Song Lyrics, or Gallery.
  2. Use the tune button for background, notifications, text size, Support, and About. Use the heart for Support.
  3. Open Quiz to practice the Pledge; Progress shows streaks and history stored on this device. The Leaderboard is optional and public.
  4. In Korean or romanised Family Pledge, use Korean recitation to play along; the list scrolls to the active verse while audio plays.
  5. While recitation or a holy song is playing, tap the mini-player title to jump between Music and the matching text screen.
  6. In Calendar, switch between list and month views and use the filter chips to show or hide event types.
  7. On phone, turn on local reminders from the tune button → Notifications, or from Calendar / Quiz.
  8. In Song Lyrics, open a song and use the chords control when available; play linked music from the same screen.
  9. While reading the Pledge or a lyric, use settings to adjust language, background, and text size.
  10. Start music, then keep browsing — the mini player stays available; lock-screen controls work on phones.
  11. Play a full playlist from Music to loop the list; study modes (lyrics play-once, Korean recitation) play without playlist loop.
  12. On Android, the system back control matches the top-left back arrow (exits only from Home).
  13. Open About (from the tune button) for the app version, store / web links, and Check for updates on iPhone and Android.
