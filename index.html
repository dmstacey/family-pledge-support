About the app

  • Read all eight Family Pledge verses in 21 languages, plus an English kids edition with a shareable PDF
  • Korean / romanised recitation: listen, follow the active verse, and jump by tapping a verse
  • Quotes & Reading — 371 quotes from True Parents’ books (coverage varies by language, including Italian and Portuguese), quote of the day, search, star favourites, optional morning reminder, share as an image, and source / audiobook links where available
  • Family Pledge Quiz — several modes, progress on your device, optional reminders, and an optional public leaderboard
  • Browse a large song lyrics library (search, language filters, and chords)
  • Listen to holy songs, piano, guitar, violin, chamber, jazz, Hyo Jin Moon piano, Keudae, Rob Mayze, Vienna Vocal Ensemble, and LA Family Church playlists
  • Search albums and songs in Music; tap the mini-player for now-playing, or tap the title to jump to matching text
  • Photo gallery
  • Heavenly calendar: holy days, anniversaries, Blessing days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times
  • Optional local reminders on iOS and Android (calendar, quiz, daily quote), including a one-time prompt when you first open the app
  • App language for menus and buttons, independent of Pledge language
  • About page with version and download links
  • Personalize background themes, text size, and card clarity
  • Also available on the web at familypledge.app

You can use the app without an account. Preferences, reminder settings, starred quotes, lyrics and songs, and quiz scores stay on your device. Sign-in (Apple, Google, or email code) is optional if you want to join the public quiz leaderboard with a display name, or to keep starred items and your background in sync across devices. You can leave the board or delete an account in the app (Account → Delete account). The App may send anonymous usage analytics to help us improve it — see the Privacy Policy.

Quick tips

  1. Start from Home to open Family Pledge, Quotes & Reading, Quiz, Calendar, Music, Song Lyrics, or Gallery.
  2. Use the tune button for app language, background, notifications, text size, Support, and About. Use the heart for Support.
  3. On a phone, the first time you open the app you may be asked to turn on reminders (calendar, quiz practice, daily quote). Fine-tune times later in Settings → Notifications.
  4. Open Quotes & Reading for quotes: quote of the day, books, themes, search, and Surprise me. Star a quote to keep it on this device. Share as an image from a quote. Book cards can open source and audiobook links.
  5. In Family Pledge, switch to the Kids edition (English) for illustrated verses. You can share the PDF from that screen.
  6. Open Quiz to practice the Pledge; Progress shows streaks and history stored on this device. The Leaderboard is optional and public.
  7. In Korean or romanised Family Pledge, use Korean recitation to play along; the list scrolls to the active verse while audio plays.
  8. While recitation or a holy song is playing, tap the mini-player title to jump between Music and the matching text screen.
  9. In Calendar, switch between list and month views and use the filter chips to show or hide event types.
  10. On phone, turn on local reminders from the first-open prompt, the tune button → Notifications, or from Calendar / Quiz / Reading.
  11. In Song Lyrics, open a song and use the chords control when available; play linked music from the same screen.
  12. While reading the Pledge or a lyric, use settings to adjust language, background, and text size.
  13. In Music, use the search field to find albums and songs. Start music, then keep browsing — the mini player stays available; lock-screen next/previous work on phones.
  14. Play a full playlist from Music to loop the list; study modes (lyrics play-once, Korean recitation) play without playlist loop.
  15. On Android, the system back control matches the top-left back arrow (exits only from Home).
  16. Open About (from the tune button) for the app version, store / web links, and Check for updates on iPhone and Android.
