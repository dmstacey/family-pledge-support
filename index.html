About the app

  • Read all eight Family Pledge verses in 20 languages
  • Browse a large song lyrics library (search, language filters, and chords)
  • Listen to holy songs, piano, guitar, chamber, and jazz playlists
  • Photo gallery
  • Heavenly calendar: holy days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times
  • Optional local reminders on iOS and Android
  • About page with version and download links
  • Personalize background themes, text size, and card clarity
  • Also available on the web at familypledge.app

No account or sign-in is required. Preferences and reminder settings are stored on your device.

Quick tips

  1. Start from Home to open Family Pledge, Calendar, Music, Song Lyrics, Gallery, Settings, or Support.
  2. In Calendar, switch between list and month views and use the filter chips to show or hide event types.
  3. On phone, use the calendar banner or Settings → Notifications to turn on local reminders for Ahn Shi Il and holy days.
  4. In Song Lyrics, open a song and use the chords control when available; play linked music from the same screen.
  5. While reading the Pledge or a lyric, use settings to adjust language, background, and text size.
  6. Start music, then keep browsing — the mini player stays available; lock-screen controls work on phones.
  7. Open Settings → About for the app version and links to the App Store, Google Play, and the web app.
