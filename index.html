About the app

  • Read all eight Family Pledge verses in 20 languages
  • Browse a large song lyrics library (search and language filters)
  • Listen to holy songs and piano music with a mini player
  • Heavenly calendar: holy days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times
  • Optional local reminders on iOS and Android
  • Personalize background themes, text size, and card clarity
  • Also available on the web at familypledge.app

No account or sign-in is required. Preferences and reminder settings are stored on your device.

Quick tips

  1. Start from Home to open Family Pledge, Calendar, Music, Song Lyrics, or Support.
  2. In Calendar, switch between list and month views and use the filter chips to show or hide event types.
  3. Open Reminders from Calendar (iOS/Android) to choose which events notify you and at what time.
  4. While reading the Pledge or a lyric, use settings to adjust language, background, and text size.
  5. Start music, then keep browsing — the mini player stays available; lock-screen controls work on phones.
