Privacy Policy

Family Pledge application (iOS, Android, and web)

Last updated: 27 August 2026

Overview

Family Pledge (“the App”) is operated by David Stacey. It helps you read the Cheon Il Guk Family Pledge, study the Korean recitation with follow-along audio, read short quotes from True Parents’ books, practice with a Family Pledge quiz, browse song lyrics (including chord charts), listen to music, view a photo gallery, and use a heavenly calendar with optional local reminders.

You can use the App without creating an account. Sign-in is optional. It is only needed if you want to join the public quiz leaderboard. This policy explains what stays on your device, what we store if you choose to sign in, and what anonymous analytics we use.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your phone number or physical address.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising in the App.
  • We do not use your data to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.
  • We do not use session replay or record your screen.
  • We do not put full pledge, lyric, or quote text into analytics events.
  • We do not upload your photos or the quote images you save.
  • We do not put your email or display name into analytics events.

Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences and settings locally on your device so your experience is remembered, including:

  • Selected app language (menus and buttons) and Family Pledge language
  • Selected background theme
  • Text size and card opacity settings
  • Last played music track (if any)
  • Starred lyrics, songs, and quotes
  • Quotes language, quote-of-the-day flags, and quote share-card preferences
  • Calendar filter preferences (which event types you show or hide)
  • Calendar, quiz, and daily-quote reminder preferences (what reminds you, and at what time)
  • Quiz preferences and local quiz history (scores and streaks)
  • One-time tip dismissals (for example mini-player, recitation, and what’s-new flags)
  • Optional offline audio cache files (on supported platforms)
  • A local sign-in session if you choose to sign in on that device

This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data through your device settings. Uninstalling the App removes local preferences and cancels scheduled local reminders on that device. It does not automatically delete an optional account on our servers — see “Leaving the leaderboard and deleting your account” below.

Optional account and sign-in

If you tap Sign in, you may use Sign in with Apple, Sign in with Google, or an email sign-in code. There are no passwords in the App.

When you sign in we receive and store:

  • An account identifier created for you
  • Your email address (from Apple, Google, or the address you type)
  • Which sign-in method you used (Apple, Google, or email)

Apple or Google may also process that sign-in under their own policies. Email codes are sent to the address you enter so you can prove you control it. Codes expire and are not used for marketing.

Signing in alone does not put you on the public leaderboard. You join only if you choose a display name and confirm.

Public quiz leaderboard

Anyone can browse the leaderboard without signing in. To appear on it you must sign in, pick a public display name (3–20 letters, numbers, spaces, or hyphens), and opt in.

If you join, we store:

  • Your display name
  • Summaries of quiz attempts used for ranking (when you finished, language and mode ids, how many questions, how many you got right)
  • Weekly and monthly totals derived from those attempts

When you join, the App may upload quiz history already stored on that device from the last 24 months so your existing practice can count. Later attempts on a signed-in device are uploaded the same way.

What other people can see: your display name, rank, number of correct answers, and accuracy for the current week or month. They cannot see your email, account identifier, or individual quiz answers.

Who processes this data

We use service providers to run sign-in, store account and leaderboard data, send email codes, host the website, stream audio, and measure anonymous usage. They process data on our behalf and are not allowed to sell it or use it for their own advertising:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple
  • Google — Sign in with Google
  • Supabase — account, display name, quiz totals, and leaderboard
  • Resend — email sign-in codes
  • Cloudflare — audio and other media delivery (R2 / CDN)
  • The host of familypledge.app — delivering the web app
  • PostHog (EU Cloud) — anonymous product analytics, described below

Those services may process ordinary technical data such as IP address as part of delivering the request. We do not use that for advertising.

Anonymous product analytics (usage data)

To understand which features are used and to improve reliability, the App sends anonymous product analytics to PostHog (EU Cloud, host eu.i.posthog.com).

Analytics are not tied to your name, email, or account. We do not call identify with personal details. Events use stable technical IDs (for example language id, track id, song id, quote id, background id), platform (iOS / Android / web), and app version — not free-form personal text. If you sign in or join the leaderboard, we may record that the action happened and which method you used (Apple, Google, or email), but not who you are.

Examples of events we may record:

  • Which section of the App you open (hub navigation / screens)
  • Which pledge language or app language you view
  • When a music track starts loading or fails to play (for error diagnosis)
  • When you open lyrics, toggle chords, or view a gallery photo
  • When you open Reading, view a quote, change quote language, or share a quote (quote id only — never the quote text)
  • When you start, finish, or leave a quiz, and reminder preference changes
  • When you change background, text size, or reminder preferences
  • When you open Progress or the leaderboard
  • When you sign in, sign out, join the leaderboard, or leave it
  • When an optional in-app update or feedback prompt is shown or answered

We do not use this data for advertising profiles. Session replay is off. PostHog processes the data as our analytics provider under their terms and privacy policy. The project is hosted in the EU region.

Notifications and reminders

On iOS and Android, you may optionally enable local calendar reminders for holy days, anniversaries, Blessing days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times, and other events you choose, optional weekly reminders for the Family Pledge quiz, and an optional daily quote reminder. Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device’s notification system. They are not sent through our servers.

The App may request notification permission (and, on some Android versions, permission related to exact alarms) only so reminders can appear at the times you configure. You can change or revoke these permissions in your device settings at any time. Reminders are not available in the web version of the App.

Internet access and media

Core reading, the quotes catalog, lyrics, themes, the calendar catalog, and many images work offline after install. Quote text stays in the App; we do not send quote bodies to our servers. Music and the Korean Family Pledge recitation stream from a content delivery network (Cloudflare R2) so the App download stays smaller. That means playback needs a network connection (or a previously cached file where offline download is available). Offline audio files stay on this device. An App update may replace those files so you receive improved recordings; they are not uploaded.

The App may also check a public version file on familypledge.app to see whether a newer store release is available. That request is a simple download of version numbers — it is not an account check.

If you sign in or open the leaderboard, the App contacts our account service (Supabase) over HTTPS.

If you share a quote, the App can create an image on your device and open the system share sheet. Destinations you pick (Messages, Mail, other apps) have their own privacy policies. If you save that image, it is written only to your on-device photo library. We do not upload it, and we do not use the photo library for anything else.

If you open external links from the App (for example Support development, Send feedback, email, or a YouTube / Spotify / website button on a music playlist), those destinations are governed by their own privacy policies. Tapping YouTube or Spotify opens the native app if it is installed, otherwise a browser page. We do not send your account, quiz, or search text to those services. Your device platform (Apple or Google) may process standard app distribution, crash, or diagnostics data according to their policies when you install or use apps from their stores.

The web version at familypledge.app is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may process standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) as part of delivering the site.

Leaving the leaderboard and deleting your account

You can Leave the leaderboard in the App. That removes your display name from the public board. You can also Sign out on a device.

To delete your account and the personal data we store for it (email, display name, uploaded quiz attempts, and leaderboard totals), open Account → Delete account in the App. You can also email support@familypledge.app from the address on the account. Quiz history that only lives on this device is not removed. Anonymous analytics events that were never tied to your account cannot be individually deleted.

Children’s privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account or joined the leaderboard, contact support@familypledge.app and we will delete that data.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
