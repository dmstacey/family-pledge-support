Privacy Policy

Family Pledge mobile application

Last updated: 11 July 2026

Overview

Family Pledge (“the App”) is a reading app for the Cheon Il Guk Family Pledge. We designed it to work primarily on your device. This policy explains what information the App uses and what it does not collect.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, or phone number through the App.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising in the App.

Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences locally on your device so your experience is remembered, including:

  • Selected language
  • Selected background theme
  • Text size and card opacity settings
  • Last played music track (if any)

This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data through your device settings.

Internet access

The App’s core features (reading pledges, themes, and bundled audio) work without requiring you to create an account. The App does not send your reading preferences to our own servers for profiling.

Your device platform (Apple or Google) may process standard app distribution, crash, or diagnostics data according to their policies when you install or use apps from their stores.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is a content-reading app and does not target children with data collection.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
