Privacy Policy

Family Pledge application (iOS, Android, and web)

Last updated: 11 August 2026

Overview

Family Pledge (“the App”) helps you read the Cheon Il Guk Family Pledge, study the Korean recitation with follow-along audio, browse song lyrics (including chord charts), listen to music, view a photo gallery, and use a heavenly calendar with optional local reminders. We designed it to work primarily on your device. This policy explains what information the App uses and what it does not collect.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or sign in.
  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or other contact details through the App.
  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not show third-party advertising in the App.
  • We do not use session replay or record your screen.
  • We do not put full pledge or lyric text into analytics events.

Information stored on your device

The App stores preferences and settings locally on your device so your experience is remembered, including:

  • Selected language
  • Selected background theme
  • Text size and card opacity settings
  • Last played music track (if any)
  • Calendar filter preferences (which event types you show or hide)
  • Calendar reminder preferences (which event types remind you, and at what time)
  • One-time tip dismissals (for example mini-player and recitation tips)
  • Optional offline audio cache files (on supported platforms)

This data stays on your device unless you delete the App or clear its data through your device settings. Uninstalling the App removes local preferences and cancels scheduled local reminders on that device.

Anonymous product analytics (usage data)

To understand which features are used and to improve reliability, the App sends anonymous product analytics to PostHog (EU Cloud, host eu.i.posthog.com).

Analytics are not tied to your name or account. There is no login and we do not call identify with personal details. Events use stable technical IDs (for example language id, track id, song id, background id), platform (iOS / Android / web), and app version—not free-form personal text.

Examples of events we may record:

  • Which section of the App you open (hub navigation / screens)
  • Which pledge language you view
  • When a music track starts loading or fails to play (for error diagnosis)
  • When you open lyrics, toggle chords, or view a gallery photo
  • When you change background, text size, or reminder preferences

We do not use this data for advertising profiles. Session replay is off. PostHog processes the data as our analytics provider under their terms and privacy policy. The project is hosted in the EU region.

Notifications and reminders

On iOS and Android, you may optionally enable local calendar reminders for holy days, Ahn Shi Il, monthly pledge times, and other events you choose. Reminders are scheduled and delivered by your device’s notification system. They are not sent through our servers.

The App may request notification permission (and, on some Android versions, permission related to exact alarms) only so reminders can appear at the times you configure. You can change or revoke these permissions in your device settings at any time. Reminders are not available in the web version of the App.

Internet access and media

Core reading, lyrics, themes, the calendar catalog, and many images work offline after install. Music and the Korean Family Pledge recitation stream from a content delivery network (Cloudflare R2) so the App download stays smaller. That means playback needs a network connection (or a previously cached file where offline download is available).

Streaming and analytics requests send ordinary technical data that networks always use (such as IP address) to the CDN and analytics hosts. We do not use that for advertising.

If you open external links from the App (for example Support development, Send feedback, or email), those destinations are governed by their own privacy policies. Your device platform (Apple or Google) may process standard app distribution, crash, or diagnostics data according to their policies when you install or use apps from their stores.

The web version at familypledge.app is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may process standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) as part of delivering the site.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. It is a content and calendar app and does not target children with data collection.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the App after updates means you accept the revised policy.
